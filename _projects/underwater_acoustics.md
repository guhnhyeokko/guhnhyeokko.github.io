## Overview

Our underwater acoustics research combines marine bioacoustics with underwater sound source localization. A primary focus is the acoustic monitoring of Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins (_Tursiops aduncus_) around Jeju Island, where signature whistles are detected from hydrophone recordings to support individual identification, population-level comparison, and monitoring of wild and rehabilitating dolphin interactions.

## Research Fields

- Dolphin acoustic monitoring and signature whistle analysis (Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphin, Jeju Island)
- Underwater sound source localization using passive small-aperture arrays
